# Privacy Policy

**Effective Date:** May 2026

At Shoaib Aftab Tech, we believe privacy is a fundamental human right. 

The **Shoaib Aftab JS Framework** is designed from the ground up to be **100% GDPR, CCPA, and ePrivacy Compliant**.

## 1. Zero Telemetry & No Tracking
Our JavaScript framework runs entirely on the client-side within the user's browser. It contains **no telemetry engines**, **no tracking pixels**, **no cookies**, and makes **zero background network requests** to our servers. By using our framework, your users' data remains completely private.

## 2. No External Dependencies
To ensure absolute privacy and security, the framework operates without reliance on external CDNs or third-party APIs. Every function, from the SPA Router to the DOM Sanitizer, is self-contained.

## 3. Contact
For privacy inquiries, please contact the Shoaib Aftab Tech security team.
